👉 It works on Xbox 360 consoles running Aurora (RGH/JTAG or other compatible exploits).

👉 Yes 👍 (any setup capable of running Aurora with script support).

👉 No. It only modifies Aurora's database (scan paths and Title Update associations).

❓ **Should I make a backup?**
👉 Yes, it's highly recommended 👍


❓ **Does it work with external hard drives or USB flash drives?**
👉 Yes, especially after cloning or migrating a storage device 👍

❓ **My game disappeared. Will this fix it?**
👉 In most cases, yes—if the issue is related to scan paths or the device ID.

❓ **Can something go wrong?**
👉 As with any database modification, there's always some risk, which is why a backup is recommended. The script also includes safeguards to help prevent crashes.

👉 Yes, after the script finishes 👍
